# Synaptic Tag-and-Capture: Primary Academic Sources

## 1) Original Frey & Morris Papers (1997-1998)

### Frey & Morris 1997 Nature
- **Title**: Synaptic tagging and long-term potentiation.
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1038/385533a0
- **PubMed**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9020359/
- **Description**: Original synaptic tagging paper showing weak TBS can tag synapses for later LTP capture

### Frey & Morris 1998 Trends Neurosci
- **Title**: Synaptic tagging: implications for late maintenance of hippocampal long-term potentiation.
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1016/s0166-2236(97)01189-2
- **PubMed**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9610879/
- **Description**: Trends review establishing synaptic tagging implications for late-LTP maintenance

### Frey & Morris 1998 Neuropharmacology
- **PubMed**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9704995/
- **Description**: Weak-before-strong dissociation: behavioral context modifies synaptic tagging

## 2) Redondo & Morris - Behavioral Tagging Review (2011)

### Redondo & Morris 2011 Nature Reviews Neuroscience
- **Title**: Making memories last: the synaptic tagging and capture hypothesis.
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn2963
- **PubMed**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21170072/
- **Description**: Landmark review that formalized the synaptic tagging and capture hypothesis as a general principle of memory consolidation.

## 3) Moncada & Viola - Behavioral Tagging + Cross-Capture

### Moncada & Viola 2007 J Neurosci
- **Title**: Induction of long-term memory by exposure to novelty requires protein synthesis: evidence for a behavioral tagging.
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1083-07.2007
- **PubMed**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17626208/
- **Description**: First evidence: novelty exposure induces behavioral tagging via protein synthesis

### Moncada et al. 2009 PNAS
- **Title**: Identification of transmitter systems and learning tag molecules involved in behavioral tagging during memory formation.
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1104495108
- **PubMed**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21768371/
- **Description**: Identifies transmitter systems and learning-tag molecules in behavioral tagging

### Ballarini et al. 2009 PNAS
- **Title**: Behavioral tagging is a general mechanism of long-term memory formation.
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0907078106
- **PubMed**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19706547/
- **Description**: Behavioral tagging as a general mechanism of long-term memory formation

### Viola et al. 2013 Prog Mol Biol Transl Sci
- **Title**: The tagging and capture hypothesis from synapse to memory.
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-420170-5.00013-1
- **PubMed**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24484708/
- **Description**: From synapse to memory: comprehensive tagging/capture review

### Moncada et al. 2014 Neural Plasticity
- **Title**: Behavioral Tagging: A Translation of the Synaptic Tagging and Capture Hypothesis.
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1155/2015/650780
- **PubMed**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26380117/
- **Description**: Behavioral tagging review: translation of synaptic tagging hypothesis

## 4) Sajikumar & Frey - Cross-Tagging + PKMzeta

### Sajikumar & Frey 2004 Neurobiol Learn Mem
- **Title**: Late-associativity, synaptic tagging, and the role of dopamine during LTP and LTD.
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nlm.2004.03.003
- **PubMed**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15183167/
- **Description**: Late-associativity, synaptic tagging, dopamine role in LTP/LTD

### Sajikumar et al. 2005 J Neurosci
- **Title**: Synaptic tagging and cross-tagging: the role of protein kinase Mzeta in maintaining long-term potentiation but not long-term depression.
- **PubMed**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15958741/
- **Description**: Synaptic tagging and cross-tagging; PKMzeta role in maintaining LTP

### Sajikumar et al. 2005 Curr Opin Neurobiol
- **Title**: Protein synthesis-dependent long-term functional plasticity: methods and techniques.
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.conb.2005.08.009

- **Description**: Protein synthesis-dependent long-term functional plasticity methods
