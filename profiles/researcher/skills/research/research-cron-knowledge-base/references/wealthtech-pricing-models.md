# Wealthtech Platform Pricing Models — Condensed Reference

Six distinct pricing models used by wealth management platforms, often in combination.

## 1. Pure AUM-Based (Bps-of-AUM)

| Platform | Rate | Avg Customer Revenue | Notes |
|----------|------|---------------------|-------|
| **Addepar** | 0.8-3 bps | ~$229K/yr | Opaque, quote-driven. ~$65K for $220M SFO. |
| **Envestnet TAMP** | 8-113 bps total | Varies | 8 bps for $2.5M+ accounts, 13 bps for $25K+. Platform fees rose 2-4 bps (2018-2022). |
| **Industry AUM bundled** | 0.96% avg (down from 1.05%) | — | Compression is in fund ERs, not advisor/platform fees. |

**Where it works:** UHNW/family offices, TAMPs, large enterprises.
**Where it struggles:** Smaller RIAs, predictable-cost seekers.

## 3. Per-User / Per-Seat

| Platform | Rate | Notes |

## 4. Freemium / Loss-Leader (Custodian-Subsidized)

| Platform | Free Features | Revenue Source |
|----------|--------------|---------------|
| **Altruist** | Portfolio accounting, reporting, rebalancing, billing, client portal | Custody fees, Model Marketplace, Altruist One premium |
| **iRebal** | Free for TD/Schwab-custodied firms | Custody fees |
| **Schwab Wealthscape** | Portfolio analytics | Custody spreads |
| **Fidelity Wealthscape** | AI-powered insights | Custody spreads |

**Where it works:** Custodians using software as customer-acquisition wedge.
**Where it struggles:** Standalone platforms competing against free alternatives.

## 5. Per-Household Premium Subscription

| Platform | Rate | Minimum | What's Included |
|----------|------|---------|-----------------|
| **Altruist One** | 0.01%/mo (1 bps) per household (~0.12%/yr) | $1/mo min per account | Enhanced cash yields (+1.20% APY sweep), Model Marketplace discounts (up to 15 bps), Tax Management suite |

**Emerging pattern:** Small per-household fee that scales with firm growth without AUM lock-in. Built-in calculators let advisors compare standard vs. premium pricing per household.

## 6. Planning Retainer / Subscription (Advisor-to-Client)

From the Envestnet | MoneyGuide + Datos Insights 2026 Fee Study:

| Metric | 2023 | 2026 | Change |
|--------|------|------|--------|
| Avg annual retainer | $4,484 | $6,815 | **+52%** |
| Monthly subscription | $215/mo | $595/mo | **+177%** |
| Flat fee (one-time) | $2,554 | $2,926 | +15% |
| AUM bundled fee | 1.05% | 0.96% | -9% |
| RIAs charging separately for planning | ~50% | **59%** | +~9pp |
| Advisors raised fees (last 12mo) | — | **53%** | — |

**Key insight:** Planning retainers surging, AUM fees compressing. The planning value stream is decoupling from AUM. This validates planning-first platforms.

## Key Takeaways for WealthForge

1. **Avoid AUM-only trap** — AUM bundled fee is the only compressing model. Price as planning subscription decoupled from AUM.
2. **Planning retainer surge (+52%) validates planning-first thesis** — Advisors can charge $6,815/yr avg for planning. A $2,400-$4,800/yr planning tool is 1-2% of a typical RIA's tech budget (which is 4.2-5% of revenue, per Schwab 2025 Benchmarking).
3. **Transparent pricing is a competitive weapon** — Advyzon's $6,500/yr published pricing vs. Addepar's opaque quote-driven pricing.
4. **Multi-tier model suggested:** Free (basic goals/aggregation) → Professional (full planning engine) → Enterprise (API, white-label, data exchange).
5. **Custodian-subsidized competition is real but narrow** — Free portfolio tools from Altruist/Schwab/Fidelity don't provide AI-driven financial planning.
