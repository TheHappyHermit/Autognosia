## OBBBA Withdrawal Plan Impact Quantification

**Channel 1: Higher Bond Yields → Lower Bond Returns**
- OBBBA adds $3.4-4.7T to deficits → higher term premium → higher bond yields → lower bond prices
- 60/40 portfolio: -0.3% to -0.5% annual return impact (bond allocation weighted)

**Channel 2: Higher Inflation → Higher Withdrawal Growth**
- 0.5pp inflation increase adds ~$625/year in withdrawals by year 10 per $1M portfolio
- 20-year $1M portfolio: ~$9,000 more in total withdrawals needed

**Channel 3: Tax Policy Changes → Direct Tax Impact**
- Estate tax exemption: $15M (2026) vs $13.99M (2025) — $1M increase
- For $14M estate: eliminates estate tax for 2026-2027 (assuming no other changes)
