# QSBS Stacking Domain Knowledge

## What is QSBS?
Qualified Small Business Stock (IRC §1202) allows exclusion of capital gains from sale of qualified small business stock from federal income tax. Pre-OBBBA: 50%/75%/100% exclusion based on holding period (5+ years).

## OBBBA Changes (effective July 4, 2025)
- **Exclusion percentages:** 50% (3yr), 75% (4yr), 100% (5yr) — replaced old 50%/75%/100% thresholds
- **Gain cap:** $15 million per issuer (lifetime) — NEW (was $10M, then $10M×5 for some)
- **Gross asset threshold:** $75 million at time of issuance (was $50M)
- **Holding period:** Still 5+ years for full exclusion
- **Stock issued after July 4, 2025** subject to new rules; pre-July stock grandfathered under old rules

## Stacking Strategies

### Multi-Issuer Stacking
- $15M gain cap is PER ISSUER — selling stakes in 5 companies = $75M total exclusion
- Each issuer must meet §1202 requirements independently
- **WealthForge opportunity:** Portfolio-level QSBS tracking dashboard showing per-issuer remaining exclusion capacity

### Trust-Based Stacking
- Placing QSBS in separate trusts can create additional exclusion capacity
- **MAJOR LIMITATION:** IRC §643(f) anti-abuse rule — IRS can collapse trusts with "substantially same grantors and beneficiaries" if tax avoidance is primary purpose
- Planning must ensure trusts have genuinely different beneficiaries or purposes
- **WealthForge opportunity:** Trust structure analyzer that flags §643(f) collapse risk

### Entity-Based Stacking
- LLCs, partnerships, and corporations can each hold QSBS independently
- Each entity's gain counts separately against its own $15M cap
- **WealthForge opportunity:** Entity structure optimizer for QSBS maximization

## Key Planning Considerations
1. **Timing:** Stock must be issued after formation of corporation (not purchased from founder)
2. **Asset test:** Gross assets must be ≤$75M at issuance and continuously after
3. **Active business:** Must be a qualified trade/business (not services, banking, farming, etc.)
4. **Holding period:** 5 years minimum — early planning critical for business exit scenarios
5. **State tax:** Most states conform to federal §1202 but some don't — check state-by-state
