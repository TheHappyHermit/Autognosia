## Market Data

- **33M** closely held businesses in US (SBA)
- **~65%** have buy-sell agreement (ABA)
- **~80%** use life insurance as funding
- **~70%** of business owners expect ownership change in 10 years (UBS/CFI)
- **~20%** have disability buy-sell provisions (ACLI)
- **~15%** include divorce as trigger (growing from <5% in 2010)
- **Businesses lose 25-50% value** without succession planning (Qooper)
- **Avg buy-sell review period: 5.2 years** (Meaden & Moore)
- **60%** of owners haven't updated in 5+ years (CFI)
- **40%** of triggering events use outdated valuation (Mercer Capital)
- **Mercer Capital recommends annual valuation updates**

## Post-Connelly Impact

Connelly v. United States (2024) — entity-purchase buy-sell with life insurance funding may create unintended estate tax exposure. Creates forced review opportunity for agreements not updated in 5+ years.

## Key IRC Sections

- **IRC §101(a):** Life insurance death benefit tax treatment
- **IRC §101(j):** Key person life insurance exceptions
- **IRC §§2701-2704:** Chapter 14 valuation rules for buy-sell
- **IRC §162:** Business expense deductibility for key person premiums
