## Three-Acquisition SMA Buildout

1. **Aperio ($1B, 2020/2021)** — Direct indexing equity capabilities. Before acquisition, BlackRock's SMA business was mostly active fixed-income. Aperio added equity DI, tax-loss harvesting, concentrated stock diversification, values-aligned screens.

2. **SpiderRock (minority 2021, full acquisition 2024)** — Customized option overlay strategies. Enables downside protection and income generation within SMAs. BlackRock SVP: "This is solely so we can better help financial advisors."

3. **Preqin ($3.2B, 2025)** — Private markets data. The "Bloomberg Terminal of private markets." Combined with eFront (2019) and Aladdin, creates integrated alts data+tech+investment stack. First integration: Aladdin private credit on Preqin (May 2026). Validates alts data as high-value infrastructure play.

## Strategic Themes

- **"Whole portfolio partner" positioning**: Managing entire portfolio for advisors across public and private markets.
- **Custom models fastest-growing**: $125B custom market, up 50% in 15 months. Avg ticket $120K vs $10K mutual funds.
- **Private markets in 401(k)**: Great Gray Trust custom glidepath (June 2025), 5-20% private allocation, 50 bps alpha projected, 38 bps fee.
- **Multi-channel distribution**: Covers RIAs (Envestnet) + TAMP (GeoWealth) + wirehouse (MS) + enterprise bank (Citi) simultaneously.

## Implications for WealthForge

1. **Do NOT compete on model portfolios** — scale advantage is insurmountable.
2. **Integrate BlackRock models as distribution channel** — planning engine → model selection → UMH execution.
3. **Custom models trend creates natural role** — WealthForge planning engine is the "model construction workbench."
4. **Private-markets-in-401(k) creates new planning requirement** — model liquidity constraints, valuation lags, fee structures.
5. **Complementary positioning**: BlackRock owns portfolio construction/execution; WealthForge owns planning/optimization; advisor owns client relationship.
