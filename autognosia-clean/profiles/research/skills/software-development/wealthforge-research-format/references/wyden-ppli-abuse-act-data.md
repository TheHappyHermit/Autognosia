# Wyden PPLI Abuse Act (S. 4279) — Key Data

**As of:** 2026-05-24
**Bill:** Protecting Proper Life Insurance from Abuse Act (S. 4279)
**Introduced:** April 13, 2026 by Sen. Ron Wyden (D-OR)
**Status:** Read twice, referred to Senate Finance Committee

## Core Mechanism

- Adds new **IRC Section 7702C** creating "Applicable Private Placement Contract" (APPC) category
- APPC = any segregated asset account under IRC §817(d) supporting multiple variable life/annuity contracts where policyholder or related parties own 25+ such contracts
- **NO grandfathering** — applies to contracts issued before, on, or after enactment
- **180-day transition window** only — exchange, convert, surrender, or liquidate within window
- After window: policy taxed on ALL prior accumulated earnings as ordinary income

## Tax Impact (No Grandfathering)

For family with $50M accumulated PPLI earnings:
- Federal ordinary income (37%): $18.5M
- NIIT (3.8%): $1.9M
- State income (CA/NY/NJ 5-13.3%): $2.5M-$6.7M
- **Total tax hit: $22.9M-$27.1M**

Liquidity crisis: requires $23M-$27M liquid cash to pay tax.

## 25-Investor Carve-Out

- Attaches to segregated asset account, NOT individual policy
- Related-party aggregation: spousal accounts, trust accounts, LLC policies all count
- Treasury has authority to prevent circumvention
- Most single-family PPLI (1-5 investors) is targeted
- Only institutional platforms (100+ investors) may qualify

## FATCA Expansion

- Amends IRC §1471: insurance companies holding themselves out as life insurers = FFIs
- Disregards IRC §953(d) election (normally exempts foreign insurers from FFI status)
- Private placement contracts and segregated accounts = financial accounts subject to FATCA
- Offshore PPLI holders face new FBAR/FATCA disclosure obligations
