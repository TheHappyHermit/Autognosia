# State Domicile Optimization — Key Data

## Dual-Track Residency System

### Track 1: Domicile (Intent-Based)
Established by intent, proven by actions. Key factors ranked by weight:
- **High:** Primary residence, driver's license (30-60 days), voter registration, address on tax returns/bank statements
- **Medium:** Home ownership/lease, social/professional ties, family location, business operations, seasonal home treatment
- **Low:** Burial plans, will/trust updates

### Track 2: Statutory Residence (Day-Count)
| State | Threshold | Abode Test | Safe Harbor |
|-------|-----------|------------|-------------|
| Most states | 183+ days | Yes | Varies |
| New York | 184+ days | Yes | 30-day de minimis (business/transit days) |
| California | No formal threshold | "Fact and circumstances" | 546-day part-year safe harbor |
| New Jersey | 184+ days | Yes | Varies |
| Massachusetts | 183+ days | Yes | Varies |

## Tiebreaker Rules (Sequential Application)
1. Permanent home location → 2. Time spent → 3. Family location → 4. Business operations → 5. Personal belongings → 6. Community participation → 7. Center of financial life → 8. Driver's license/voter registration → 9. Political participation → 10. Declaration of domicile

## Breakeven Formula

```
Net_Annual_Benefit = (Old_rate - New_rate) × (Income + Capital_Gains + Retirement + Business)
                    - SALT_deduction_loss - Additional_property_tax - Cost_of_living_adjustment

Breakeven_Years = Total_Cost / Net_Annual_Benefit
```

**Example:** $50M net worth, $10M income, CA → FL: $1.33M/year savings, breakeven ~0.1 years, NPV over 20 yrs at 5% = $17.2M

## Compliance Documentation Timeline

**Pre-Move (90-180 days):** Legal opinion, Declaration of Domicile, driver's license transfer, voter registration, bank account moves, IRS address change, will/trust review

**During Transition (12 months):** Daily day-count log, primary residence docs, secondary home treatment, address consistency, part-year filing, evidence of intent

**Post-Move (ongoing):** Annual reaffirmation, <183-day visits, visit documentation, new-state presence maintenance, trust/estate alignment

## Key Edge Cases

- **Military:** MSRRA allows spouse to maintain domicile regardless of location
- **Remote work nexus:** Working for origin-state employer may not protect against origin-state taxation
- **Spousal domicile:** Community property rules may force one spouse to follow
- **Trust vs personal domicile:** Dynasty trusts can provide state tax benefits without personal domicile change
- **SALT deduction loss:** Federal tax increase from losing SALT deduction (capped at $10K)
- **Cost of living offset:** Some zero-tax states have higher property taxes/COL that partially offset savings

## Competitive Landscape

Zero platforms (eMoney, RightCapital, MoneyGuidePro, Orion, Addepar, Black Diamond) model domicile change as a strategic planning option. All treat state tax as a static input.
