**Compounding insight:** Each 5-year age increase adds ~1.0% mortality credit. The rate of increase diminishes with age (non-linear).

## State Guaranty Association Limits

Standard NAIC Model Act limits for annuity present value:

| State Group | Limit (Present Value) | Notes |
|-------------|----------------------|-------|
| Most states | $250,000 | Standard NAIC model limit |
| CA, TX, FL, NY | $500,000 | Higher limits in large states |
| WA, OR, IL | $250,000 | Standard |
| All states | $100,000 | Death benefit component |

**Concentration rule:** For premiums >$250K, split across 3+ carriers to stay within guaranty limits per carrier per state.

**Carrier insolvency context:** 2005-2008 saw multiple failures (American General, Conseco, Colonial Life). Guaranty system paid out but coverage is capped.

## SPIA vs DIA Comparison

| Feature | SPIA | DIA |
|---------|------|-----|
| Income start | Immediate | Deferred (5-15 years) |
| Initial payout rate | 6.5-10.8% | 4-6% (deferred growth) |
| Payout rate at start | 7.8% (age 65) | 12%+ (age 75 start) |
| Interest rate risk | Locked in | Mitigated by deferral |
| Liquidity | Zero | Deferred growth phase |
| Best for | Immediate income floor | Maximizing lifetime income |

**Breakeven:** DIA typically beats SPIA for clients who live past age 80. SPIA wins for clients with <80% probability of reaching age 85 (health-adjusted).
