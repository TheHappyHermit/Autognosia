### Implications

- **First-mover advantage**: Any platform that provides per-document OCR quality metrics gains a unique differentiator in compliance software.
- **Regulatory tailwinds**: SEC/FINRA increasingly require audit trails for document processing. Quality metrics provide that trail.
- **WealthForge positioning**: This fits WealthForge's "compliance-first" architecture — making quality visible is a feature, not a bug.
