## Key Planning Implications

- **NY 105% cliff:** Estate of $7,350,001 owes tax on entire estate from first dollar. Estate of $7,717,499 is safe. Estate of $7,717,500+ owes tax on everything.
- **NY example:** $7,600,000 estate → $1,012,000 NY tax. $7,750,000 estate → $1,240,000 NY tax (cliff triggered).
- **IL example:** $4,000,001 estate → entire estate taxed at 0.8-16%. $3,999,999 → $0 tax.
- **OR example:** $1,000,001 estate → entire estate taxed at 0.8-16%. $999,999 → $0. Lowest exemption in US.
- **Federal vs state gap:** $15M federal exemption vs $1M Oregon = $14M planning gap for OR residents.
- **Federal portability does NOT apply to state estate tax** in most states.
- **3-year gift clawback:** NY, IL, and others pull back gifts made within 3 years of death.
- **Maryland has BOTH estate AND inheritance tax** — unique among all states.
