# Studio Pit Sheet — DRAFTS (Ben/Rebeka)

**Scene:** Ben drafts Rebeka for a corporate sting. He's done this before; she's the first who saw the board playing both sides and lived to say it out loud. The severance is real, the NDA is plain text, the handoff is one evening. No chase, no meet-cute, no third act twist. The cost is the plot.

**Characters:**
- Ben — late 30s or early 40s, tailored but tired, the kind of man whose authority lives in what he doesn't say.
- Rebeka — sharp, practical, no sentimentality. She comes in knowing roughly what the price is; what she doesn't know is how much of it is already paid.

**Tone:** Quiet menace. Compromise as a slow bruise. Class tension without caricature. The horror is ordinary.

**Leaning:** Indie-indie / genre-bent — literary interiority rendered through concrete image. Think autumn light on wet pavement, fluorescent hum, the exact weight of a folder in a coat pocket. No soft glow. No third-act glow.

**Constraints:**
- 4 renders: BRIEF, COVER, COVER2, AFTERMATH
- Aspect 16:9 (also crop-friendly for covers)
- Consistent palette across all 4: cold exteriors (steel blue, sodium orange, wet asphalt grey), interiors (fluorescent green-grey, one sharp artificial accent), skin/wardrobe restrained, minimal palette per frame
- Texture: gritty film grain is welcome; keep it grounded, not cinematic polish
- Mood: restraint, compromise, ordinary menace — not melodrama
- No soft bloom, no over-smoothing, no fantasy lighting

**Color & texture:**
- BRIEF: cold blue-hour exterior, one amber practical, snow/rime texture, low key
- COVER: glass, frosted glass, dead monitor, neon bleed on one cheek, polished table reflection, corporate green-grey
- COVER2: wet asphalt, sodium orange puddle reflections, soaked wool, streetlight flare kept minimal and practical
- AFTERMATH: porcelain white/grey basin, red ring glint only, paper texture, dawn cold light, matte surfaces

**Render approach:**
- Midjourney v6, `--style raw`, moderate stylize, seeds fixed per image for consistency
- Light: practical sources, hard chiaroscuro where appropriate, shallow DOF on subjects, environmental storytelling
- Composition: keep subject readable, let environment carry weight, avoid clutter

**One-liner:** The severance was generous, the NDA was plain text, and Rebeka was the first recruit who asked what the board was hiding.

**Title options:**
- DRAFTS
- THE HANDOFF
- A JOB THAT PAYS ONCE
- THE FIRST RECRUIT
- COMPROMISE AS A SLOW BRUISE
- ORDINARY MENACE
- BEN DRAFTS REBEKA
- THE COST IS THE PLOT
