## 🔬 Science

**Psilocybin leaves brain changes detectable for a month.** A single dose of psilocybin produced measurable shifts in brain activity and possible structural changes lasting up to a month in a UCSF/Imperial College study in *Nature Communications*. Participants with the largest spike in brain "entropy" (more varied neural activity) reported more personal insight and better well-being a month later — evidence that the psychedelic experience itself, not just the chemistry, may drive therapeutic benefit for depression, anxiety and addiction.

**First puffling seen on Dorset coast in three decades.** Volunteers spotted a baby puffin on the Purbeck coast, the last known regular puffin nesting site on mainland southern England. The colony had fallen to just three pairs with no confirmed fledglings for 30 years; the chick is a sign the site may still be viable, though conservationists note one bird won't save it.

**Australian cave shows 25,000 years of ritual.** Layers of grass ash in Cloggs Cave, GunaiKurnai country in Victoria, span more than 1,000 generations of magic, healing and cursing ceremonies. Phytolith analysis shows people deliberately carried whole grasses — roots and all — into the cave to burn for ritual, doubling the known timespan of continuous cultural practice at the site.
