## Core Differentiator: "Three-Pillar Simultaneous Optimization"

1. **Dynamic Withdrawal Sequencing** — Annual re-optimization (NOT set-and-forget). Tests thousands of scenarios, delivers curated list. Roth conversion + IRMAA/Medicare integrated.
2. **SSAnalyzer (bundled)** — Top-ranked Social Security tool (T3 surveys). Handles divorced/widowed/working scenarios. Social Security Zone™ visualization across all life expectancies.
3. **Active Investment Management** — Household rebalancing, asset location, model portfolio/annuity marketplace with "Easy Button" trade recommendations.

## Key Claim: "7 Extra Years or $1M After Taxes"

- Study of **5,700+ households** (retail advisory service users)
- **Proprietary self-research** (not independently audited like EY/LifeYield)
- "Conventional approach" baseline undefined in public materials
- Format: behind-registration white paper

## Critical Market Insight (from Income Lab's comparison page)

> *"Free tools solve the technical problem: what is the optimal withdrawal sequence? Paid specialist tools solve the practice problem: how do I build a retirement income service that differentiates my firm, retains clients, and justifies my fee?"*

**Income Solver = technical problem. Income Lab = practice problem. Neither solves both.** WealthForge's opportunity.

## Threat/Risk Assessment

- **THREAT:** T. Rowe Price cross-subsidizes pricing via fund fees; independent platforms can't match $1,900/yr if T. Rowe Price decides to offer it at cost.
- **THREAT:** T. Rowe Price's distribution network (millions of advisor relationships) can rapidly drive adoption.
- **OPPORTUNITY:** Asset-manager-owned software creates real/perceived fund conflicts — WealthForge's independence is a credible differentiator.
- **OPPORTUNITY:** T. Rowe Price's 3-year latency from acquisition to launch suggests building a comprehensive planning engine is hard even for a $1.78T firm.
- **PRICING CEILING:** Income Solver's $1,900-$5,000/yr sets WealthForge's pricing ceiling for a retirement module.
